Metric
Make a route the preferred route by changing its metric. In this example, cost has been configured to make R3 the next hop for R1.
The default metric on each interface is 10. Interface eth2 on R2 has a metric of 20, and Interface eth2 on R3 has a metric of 30. The total cost to reach 3ffe:10::/64 (R4) through R2 and R3:
R2: 10+20 = 30
R3: 10+30 = 40
In this topology, R1 chooses R2 as its next hop for destination 10.10.14.0/24.

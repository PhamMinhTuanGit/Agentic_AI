CHAPTER 27 MPLS-TP BFD for LSPs
This chapter provides examples of configuring and validating Bidirectional Forwarding Detection (BFD) setups. The examples are based on co-routed tunnel configurations. For details about configuring a co-routed tunnel, refer to Co-Routed Bi-Directional Tunnel. For information about co-routed connectivity topology, refer to Figure 20-2.

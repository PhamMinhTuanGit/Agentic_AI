Virtual Routing Command Modes
The Global Management Authority (GMA) resides in the router and provides services to each VR management authority (VRMA), but does not communicate on its own to resources outside the router.
Global Administrators log on to the GMA and virtual router administrators log on to the VRMA using similar commands. The GMA allows users to log on to any VR.

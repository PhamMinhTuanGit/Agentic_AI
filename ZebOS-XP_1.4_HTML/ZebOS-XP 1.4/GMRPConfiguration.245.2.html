Topology
In this example, bridges 2 is forwarding multicast packets coming from the Host to the gmrp-enabled bridge 3. To configure GMRP on a bridge, enable spanning tree on the bridge, disable IGMP snooping, and associate interfaces with the bridge group. Then enable GMRP on all ports for the bridge.

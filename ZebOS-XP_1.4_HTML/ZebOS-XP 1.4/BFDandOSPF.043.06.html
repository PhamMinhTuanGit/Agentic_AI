Messages
The following messages are exchanged between the BFD client and the BFD OSPF management interfaces:
• BFD_MSG_SESSION_ADD
• BFD_MSG_SESSION_DELETE
• BFD_MSG_SESSION_UP
• BFD_MSG_SESSION_DOWN
• BFD_MSG_SESSION_ERROR
• BFD_MSG_SESSION_ATTR_UPDATE

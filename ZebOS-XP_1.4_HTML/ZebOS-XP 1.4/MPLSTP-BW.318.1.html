CHAPTER 22 MPLS-TP Tunnel Bandwidth
Quality of service (QoS) is the ability to assign different priorities to different applications, users or data flows, or to guarantee a certain level of performance to a data flow. Bandwidth is a factor of QoS. This chapter describes configuring and then validating the bandwidth of a bidirectional tunnel.

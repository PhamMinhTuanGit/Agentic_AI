One-way and Two-way Delay Measurement
Support to generate and process one-way and two-way delay measurement (1DM and 2DM) PDUs is not provided in the control plane and must be implemented in hardware.
PE1 (OAM)
 
#configure terminal
Enter configure mode.
(config)#itut meg meg-c
Configure the MEG name as meg-c.
(config-itut-meg)#service type tunnel
Configure the service type as tunnel.
(config-itut-meg)#mep-id 1
Configure the MEP identifier as 1.
(config-itut-mp)#service tunnel tnl_co
Associate tnl1_co with the ME.
(config-itut-mp)#1dm interval 4
Configure one way delay measurement interval to 600 seconds.
(config-itut-mp)#2dm interval 4
Configure two way delay measurement interval to 600 seconds.
(config-itut-mp)#rmep-id 4 cc IN icc AIRTEL umc meg-c
Configure the remote MEP.

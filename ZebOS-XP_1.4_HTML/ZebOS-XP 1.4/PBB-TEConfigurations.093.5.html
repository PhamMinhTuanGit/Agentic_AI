Verify Switchover Process
Set eth1 to down on IB-BEB-1 to check that switchover takes place correctly when the working-tesi goes down.
IB-BEB-1(config)#interface eth1
IB-BEB-1(config-if)#shutdown
IB-BEB-1(config-if)#end
Verify switchover using the following commands:
IB-BEB-1#show pbb-te aps-group all
 
Bridge ApsID Working-Path Protection-Path Current-State Active-Path
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
backbone 1 10 20 Working SF 20
 
IB-BEB-1#show pbb-te aps-group all
 
Bridge ApsID Working-Path Protection-Path Current-State Active-Path
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
backbone 1 10 20 No-Request 20
Note: After eth1 of IB-BEB-1 is shut down, the Active path becomes 20, which is the protection-tesi.

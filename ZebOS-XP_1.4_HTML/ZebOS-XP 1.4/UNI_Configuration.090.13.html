MEF 10.2 Configuration
This section shows how to enable MEF10.2 on a bridge and interface.
MEF 10.2 introduced the concept of a rooted-multipoint EVC as shown in Figure 8-2.
Figure 8-2: Rooted Multipoint EVC
The User Network Interface (UNI) is the physical demarcation point between the responsibility of the Service Provider and the responsibility of the Subscriber. An Ethernet Virtual Connection (EVC) is an association of two or more UNIs. A given UNI can support more than one EVC via the Service Multiplexing attribute.
There are three types of EVCs:
• In a point-to-point EVC, exactly two UNIs are associated with one another.
• In a multipoint EVC, two or more UNIs are associated with one another
• In a rooted-multipoint EVC, one or more of the UNIs must be designated as root and each of the other UNIs must be designated as a leaf. If root, the UNI can send service frames to all other points in the EVC; if leaf, the UNI can send and receive service frames to and from root only.

NSM_MSG_BRIDGE_DEL_G8032_VLAN

NSM_MSG_BRIDGE_DEL_PG

NSM_MSG_LACP_AGGREGATOR_CONFIG
Sent from NSM to protocols for configuration of LACP aggregator
NSM_MSG_LACP_DEL_AGGREGATOR_MEMBER

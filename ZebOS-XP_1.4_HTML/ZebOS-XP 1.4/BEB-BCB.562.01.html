CHAPTER 1 Shortest Path Bridging - MAC Configuration
This chapter shows how to configure backbone edge bridges (BEBs) and backbone core bridges (BCBs) for Shortest Path Bridging - MAC (SPBM).
The boundary between the core MAC-in-MAC SPBM domain and the edge customer 802.1Q domain is handled by BEBs. BEBs can contain an I-Component or B-Component or both an I-Component and B-Component:
• The I-Component maps Service VLAN identifiers (S-VIDs) to service instance identifiers (I-SIDs) and adds a PBB header without a B-Tag.
• The B-Component maps I-SIDs to backbone VIDs (B-VIDs) and adds a PBB header with a B-Tag.
In this document, “BEB” refers to a backbone edge bridge having both I and B components.
BCBs act as transit nodes, forwarding packets based on outer VLAN identifier (B-VID) and destination MAC address (B-DA).

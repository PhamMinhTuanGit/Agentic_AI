Validation 2
To disable flood reduction on a particular interface, use the below command. On disabling flood reduction DNA bit should be removed from database and LSA refresh should take place at regular intervals.
#conf ter
Enter configuration commands, one per line. End with CNTL/Z.
(config)#int eth2
(config-if)#no ipv6 ospf flood-reduction
(config-if)#end

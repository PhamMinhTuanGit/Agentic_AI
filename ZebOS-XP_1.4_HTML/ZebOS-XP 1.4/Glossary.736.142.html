Glossary : Terms : end-of-row switch
  
end-of-row switch
A chassis-based switch in a rack or cabinet at either end of the server row in a data center that connects to hundreds of servers in that row. Each cabinet in the row has cabling connecting 48 (or more) servers to the end-of-row switch. An end-of-row switch typically has redundant supervisor engines, power supplies, and overall better high availability characteristics than a Top-of-Rack (ToR) switch.
An end-of-row switch extends Layer 1 (L1) cabling topology from the switch to each rack, resulting in a smaller Layer 2 (L2) footprint and fewer Spanning Tree Protocol (STP) nodes in the topology.

Validation
Use the commands listed in the sections below to verify and validate the configurations.
Verify CFM Maintenance Points
On IB-BEB-1
IB-BEB-1#show ethernet cfm pbb-te maintenance-points remote domain-name abc te-sid 10 bridge backbone

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
70 3 10 Yes 0005.1111.1212 True
IB-BEB-1#

Verify CFM Traceroute
On IB-BEB-1
IB-BEB-1#traceroute pbb-te ethernet esp-da-mac 0005.1111.1212 domain-name abc te-sid 10 tlv pbb-te-mip reverse-vid 4 bridge backbone

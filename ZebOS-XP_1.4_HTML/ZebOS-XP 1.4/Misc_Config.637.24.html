Multicast Route Information
RBridges build distribution trees and use these trees for forwarding multi-destination frames.Each distribution tree reaches all RBridges in the campus, is shared across all VLANs, and may be used for the distribution of a native frame that is in any VLAN.However, the distribution of any particular frame on a distribution tree is pruned in different ways for different cases to avoid unnecessary propagation of the frame.
Use the same topology and configuration as in Chapter 17, Multicast OAM.

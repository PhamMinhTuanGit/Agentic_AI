Carrier Ethernet Configuration Guide : CHAPTER 10 Ethernet Protection Switching Configuration : Removing Ethernet Protection Switching : Bridge 1
  
Bridge 1
 
(config)#g8031 configure switching eps-id 10 bridge 1
Enter G8031 Configure Switching mode.
(config)#no g8031 ethernet md-name test service-id eps
Remove the association of EPS group 10 with the CFM domain and MA..
(g8031-config-switching)#no g8031 mode
Reconfigure the switching mode to no g8031 mode.
(g8031-config-switching)#exit
Exit G8031 Configure Switching mode.
(config)#no bridge 1 g8031 eps-id 10
Delete EPS protection group 10.

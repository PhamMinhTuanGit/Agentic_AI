Configuration for R1
 
R1#configure terminal
Enter the Configure mode.
R1(config)#router bgp 100
Enter the Router mode for BGP.
R1(config-router)#neighbor 3.3.3.3 remote-as 100
Add the neighbor 3.3.3.3/32 to remote-as 100
R1(config-router)#neighbor 3.3.3.3 fall-over bfd multihop
Enable the BFD option for the neighbor.

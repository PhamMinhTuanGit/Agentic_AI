CHAPTER 11 Access Port
TRILL supports multi-access LAN links that can have multiple end stations and RBridges attached. Appointed Forwarders handle the native traffic to and from end stations on that link, with the intent that native traffic in each VLAN be handled by at most one port of one RBridge. Enabling access option on port will allow port to send or receive normal L2 frames and to discard TRILL encapsulated frames.

Extract ZebOS-XP Modules
1. Change to the directory where you want to store the ZebOS-XP source:
cd /myZebOSpath
2. Extract the ZebOS-XP source software from the.tar file:
tar -xvzf TARFILE
where TARFILE is the name of the ZebOS-XP .tar file.

DOT1.X Tunneling in UNI
UNI can be configured to tunnel the Layer 2 protocol DOT1.X. When DOT1.X is present, a Tunneling xsupplicant on one side of a Provider Network gets authenticated by RADIUS server, which is in other side of the Provider Network. The customer-edge port (UNI) of the Provider Network is configured to tunnel DOT1.X packets.

Figure 5-42: Basic Topology for Indication LSA Generation

Validation
Verify R2 Area Border Router has generated an Indication LSA (I LSA) for area 0 and no indication LSA should be generated for area1 since demand circuit incapable router is in area1.
In the Inter-area Router LSA. the term ‘I LSA’ indicates that R2 (2.2.2.2) has generated Indication LSA for R1.

Carrier Ethernet Configuration Guide : CHAPTER 7 MEF ENNI Configuration : Layer 2 Control Protocol : EPL, EP-LAN, and EP-TREE Services
  
EPL, EP-LAN, and EP-TREE Services
EPL, EP-TREE and EP-LAN must process the L2CP frames based on the Destination MAC address as specified in the following table.

01-80-C2-00-00-00
MUST Tunnel
01-80-C2-00-00-01 through 01-80-C2-00-00-0A
MUST NOT Tunnel (additional requirements may apply as per the sub-section 8.1.1, 8.1.3, 8.1.5)
01-80-C2-00-00-0B
MUST Tunnel
01-80-C2-00-00-0C
MUST Tunnel
01-80-C2-00-00-0D
MUST Tunnel
01-80-C2-00-00-0E
MUST Tunnel
01-80-C2-00-00-00F
MUST NOT Tunnel (additional requirements may apply as per the sub-section 8.1.1, 8.1.3, 8.1.5)

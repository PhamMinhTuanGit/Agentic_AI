3. Verify whether CVLAN registration table is not getting modified in contradiction to configuration of UNI to which it is associated. Modify CVLAN registration table (map1 of PB1) and try mapping CVLAN 4 to SVLAN 30.
Map1 should not get modified as it is associated to customer-edge port (eth0 of PB1), which is configured for bundling.
PB1#configure terminal
Enter configuration commands, one per line. End with CNTL/Z.
PB1(config)#cvlan registration table map1 bridge 1
PB1(config-cvlan-registration)#cvlan 4 svlan 30

cc interval
Use this command to set the continuity checking (CC) interval.
Command Syntax
cc (vlan VLAN_ID|) (interval (1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7)) (bridge <1-32>|)
Parameters
vlan
VLAN ID.
interval
The CC interval:
1
3 milliseconds.
2
10 milliseconds.
3
100 milliseconds.
4
1 seconds.
5
10 seconds.
6
1 minute.
7
10 minutes.
bridge
Bridge ID.
Command Mode
Ethernet CFM configuration mode
Examples
(config-ether-cfm)#cc vlan 5 interval 4 bridge 16

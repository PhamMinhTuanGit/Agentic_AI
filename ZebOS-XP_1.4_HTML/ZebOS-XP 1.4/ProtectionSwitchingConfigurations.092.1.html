CHAPTER 10 Ethernet Protection Switching Configuration
Ethernet Protection Switching (EPS) describes the specifics of protection switching for Ethernet VLAN signals. Protection switching is a fully allocated survivability mechanism, in the sense that the route and bandwidth of the protection entity is reserved for a selected working entity.
The configurations in this section relate to the simple topology below, and are for creating and configuring an Ethernet Protection Switching group.

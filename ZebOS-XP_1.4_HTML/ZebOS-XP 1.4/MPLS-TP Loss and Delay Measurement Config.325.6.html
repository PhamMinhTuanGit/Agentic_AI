Multi-Protocol Label Switching Configuration Guide : CHAPTER 29 MPLS-TP Loss and Delay Measurement : Proactive Delay Measurement
  
Proactive Delay Measurement
PE1 - OAM
 
#configure terminal
Enter configure mode.
(config)#ietf meg meg1
Configure MEG name as meg1.
(config-ietf-meg)#me me1 tunnel
Configure ME name as me1
(config-ietf-me)#service tunnel tunnel-1
Configure ME as associated with tunnel-1.
(config-ietf-me)#delay-measurement
Initiate proactive delay-measurement session for meg1 and me1. Interval can be configured in the range of <1-4>.
